# Artwork generation prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Source images are preserved unchanged. The preview renderer crops and scales the transparent sprites into 100 × 128 cells.

## Digits

Create a production sprite sheet for a light-mode glass digital clock, using the attached photo ONLY as the material and numeral-shape reference. Match its thick rounded clear glass, pale silver-blue translucent interiors, double refractive beveled edges, icy blue rim light at upper left and tiny champagne warm highlights at bottom/right. Numerals should look like the reference: very bold rounded sans-serif, clean recognizable shapes, almost front-on orthographic with a small visible glass depth. NOT metal/chrome, NOT black interiors, NOT thin outline digits.
Output a landscape 1536x1024 image with a REAL transparent alpha background, no floor, no ground reflections, no contact shadows outside the objects, no labels. Exactly a rigid 6-column by 2-row grid of equal 256x512 cells. Top row has digits 0 1 2 3 4 and 5. Bottom row has digits 6 7 8 9 then a colon : in column five, with column six EMPTY. Each numeral fully contained in its cell, centered horizontally and vertically, same cap height 340 px, baseline at row-local y=426, top at y=86. Max width 214 px. Transparent holes in 0 6 8 9 and 4. Colon is two glass spheres about 65 px across, centered vertically as a pair. Keep consistent lighting, scale, material, perspective across all eleven glyphs. Interiors should have pale blue-gray luminous cloudy glass shading and mostly opaque enough to be visible over a light background; only outside silhouettes and holes are transparent. No clipping. No extra text. These exact raster glyphs will be extracted into clock firmware.

A luminous pearl-white and extremely pale powder-blue studio interior, soft large blurred window-frame shadows diagonally falling across a pale blue-white wall at upper left and extending as soft horizontal streaks on the glossy tabletop/floor. Smooth seamless wall-to-floor studio gradient with the floor occupying the bottom third; subtle transition near y=210 of 320 (65.6% down). Background center and area where digits will appear (x=20..460 y=80..212) are light and subtly blue, uncluttered. White sunny daylight enters from upper left, cool blue tint to the right, tiny hint of cream warmth lower left. Reflective white polished floor, but no reflected objects because scene is empty. High key, realistic architectural studio photograph. Match the supplied photo as closely as possible after removing all digits and their reflections. No obvious hard horizon line, no borders or graphic decorations.
